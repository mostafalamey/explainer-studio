# Flat-Vector Science

Use `assets/styles/flat-vector-science/runtime.js` through `STYLE_PACKS['flat-vector-science']`. The complete user-supplied reference is preserved beside it. Study the source's scale changes and finishing; its AI story and animals are examples, not default content.

## Visual language

Deep navy-to-violet gradients, seeded parallax stars and soft radial nebula glows. Use chunky outline-free silhouettes with rich multi-stop gradients, two stepped crescent shadows and a thin lit rim. Preserve `ball`, `crescent`, `sky`, `glow`, `spark`, rounded `pill`, dotted cubic `arrow` and offset-shadow `text`. For noncircular subjects, adapt the same gradient, clipped flat shadow and rim construction to accurate subject geometry.

Use rounded Nunito 900 for headlines and key numbers, Nunito 700 for essential labels. Fredoka is bundled as the reference's alternate. Fonts are embedded with their OFL notices. Logical sizes: headlines at least 44px, key numbers 64px, labels and corner headers 28px. Numbers should be focal objects with strong contrast. Keep genuine logos separate from illustrative shapes.

## Motion and explanation

Ease camera pullbacks and dives to explain relationships and scale; use gentle overshoot for arrivals, small character bobs and pulsing glows. `sky` accepts the camera position/zoom for parallax. Read all semantic cues from the project beat/event times. Keep connected ideas in one scene, and use a foreground subject sweep or glow dive only when the story requires a change. Never add planets, brains, animals or scientific quantities merely because they appeared in the source.

## Finishing and sound

Run `finish(ctx, 1280, 720, t)` every frame, unchanged: static mottling at 0.12 overlay alpha and a seeded tile at 0.09, shifting on a 24fps clock. Cache textures but do not remove these finishing layers. Other random behavior must be seeded and seekable.

Use rounded boops, low-mid marimba-like plucks, gentle call chimes and airy whooshes for camera travel. Minor cues sit below the voice; no music bed is needed for the narrated comparison. Visual and sound reveals share event times.

The included kiosk-to-reception sample uses the approved 8.3-second Smart Queue excerpt and existing English voice, with an illustrative G-001 ticket. It demonstrates a continuous pullback from kiosk to ticket to reception, not a universal layout.
