# Isometric Pastel

Load `assets/styles/isometric-pastel/runtime.js` through the builder. Its helpers are exposed as `STYLE_PACKS["isometric-pastel"]`. The unchanged `reference.js` includes both the hero frame and travelling factory explanation.

True 30-degree isometric projection; pastel lavender, mint and cream with coral accent; three-face shading, subtle tonal falloff, contact occlusion, soft long shadows and a floating foundation. Inter typography, white pinned labels, dashed curved connectors, generous framing.

Use `makeIso`, `box`, `shade`, `poly`, `cylinder`, `tag`, `numberTag`, `arrow`, `wallX` and `floorX`. These are Canvas illustrations, not a WebGL dependency. Build subject-specific objects. The factory, cats/dogs and AI example are illustrative, not a fixed template.

Plan world positions, painter's draw order, camera focal points and the path of the recurring subject before animating. Scale and translate the camera to reveal the next area; retain spatial continuity. Use springy assembly, modest floating movement and purposeful small activity. Keep essential labels upright in screen space where projection would impair reading.

Override reference helper font defaults: `tag` defaults to 13px and `numberTag` uses a 24px subtitle. Supply or adapt them to meet the shared 28px essential-label rule. Reference miniature decoration need not be promoted to story text.

Preserve face shading and contact/shadow finishing. Never add Tech Glow's bloom or neon grid. Share camera keyframes between drawing and sound positioning. Use soft block taps, low pops, gentle air movement and restrained confirmation tones beneath narration.
